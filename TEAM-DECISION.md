## The distinction that decides it

Teammates and study participants are different problems with different rules.

- A **teammate** is a classmate. Fine for a teammate, disqualifying for a participant.
- A **participant** must be non-cohort (`CAPSTONE-RULES.md` A1, confirmed by 100x 1 Sep).

So adding a peer does not touch the only thing that is actually unsolved. The 8-10 non-cohort
people are still needed by 6 Sep either way. The single point of failure does not move.

That alone removes the strongest argument for recruiting.

## Where a second person genuinely would have paid

Not the build. Scope is deliberately boring and Vikrant is a 10-yr RN dev; marginal code
throughput is not the constraint. The real cost centre is the **study operations**: two arms,
8-10 people, practice 21 Sep, removal test 27 Sep, scoring 28-29 Sep. Running both arms solo is
the operationally hard part of this project. That upside is real and was weighed.

## Why it still fails the bar

1. **Selection is adversarial on 2 Sep.** The session is a room of people optimising for not
   being alone on 7 Oct. Whoever is unteamed by that evening is selected for availability, not
   motivation. That is precisely the pool Vikrant said he does not want.
2. **The bar cannot be verified in ~40 hours.** Motivated / contributing / complementary are
   all observable over weeks, not over one networking session before the form window closes
   (2 Sep 23:59).
3. **Grade coupling.** Team capstones are graded as a team. The two highest-risk dates
   (6 Sep scope lock, 30 Sep freeze) are fully under Vikrant's control today. A teammate who
   fades on 25 Sep, four days before the analysis window, is strictly worse than no teammate.
4. **Unspecified interface.** An interface is a shared agreement. There is no time to negotiate
   one three weeks before a hard experimental protocol. Shipping an unspecified interface with
   a human on the other end is the risk being declined.
5. **Solo has a small structural bonus.** Rule C requires the thin slice be "run by someone not
   on your team." Solo, everyone qualifies.
6. Team size is not scored. COMPLEXITY IS NOT CREDIT applies to headcount too.

## What 2 Sep is for instead

Unchanged from `RECRUITMENT.md`:
1. **Mentor conversation** — get the rewritten hypothesis checked. Rule B1, still unchecked,
   and it is step one of the FAQ's own sequence. This is the highest-value item in the room.
2. **Referrals** — ask cohort members for their non-technical friends, never for the person.
3. **Reciprocal help, no grade coupling** — optionally agree with another solo builder to help
   run each other's sessions. Hands without shared fate. This is the only "peer" arrangement
   worth making.
