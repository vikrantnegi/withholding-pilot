## 7. Four rules you must not break

- **Never say "arm", or that there are two versions of the app.** They are testing a SQL practice
  tool. `../../RECRUITMENT.md` §3 — never let one version read as "his".
- **One link per person, sent privately.** Never in a group chat. Two people comparing links is
  how they find out the versions differ.
- **If someone loses their link, resend the same one.** A second code for the same person makes
  two logs that look like two people.
- **Say the voluntary line out loud, not just in writing.** `../../RECRUITMENT.md` §2 — four of these
  seven were on your team, and the consent rule needs restating at the session, not only at
  recruitment.

**So what:** the first rule protects the comparison, the second and third protect the data, and
the fourth protects the consent the whole study rests on.
